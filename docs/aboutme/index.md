#About Me

My name is Aiden Grake, and I am a Mechanical and Motorsports Engineering student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I chose to pursue Mechanical and Motorsports Engineering because of my passion for motorsports and my interest in the engineering that makes high-performance vehicles possible. Motorsports provides a unique environment where engineering, physics, manufacturing, and design come together to solve complex problems and improve vehicle performance.


I enjoy learning how different components and systems within the motorsports industry operate and understanding how they can be improved. One of the aspects of engineering that interests me most is how a small design decision can have a significant impact on the overall performance, reliability, or efficiency of a vehicle. This has made me especially interested in the mechanical design, CAD, physics, manufacturing processes, problem-solving, and engineering analysis involved in developing motorsports systems.


Throughout my engineering education, I have worked to develop both my technical knowledge and practical skills. I have gained experience with CAD and mechanical design while also developing an understanding of how engineering concepts are applied to real-world problems. Hands-on projects have allowed me to strengthen my ability to design, analyze, and evaluate mechanical components while considering factors such as performance, manufacturability, and reliability. These experiences have also taught me the importance of approaching engineering problems systematically and using analysis and evidence to support design decisions.


As I continue my education, I hope to further develop my engineering skills and gain experience working on increasingly complex mechanical and motorsports projects. I am interested in applying what I learn in the classroom to practical engineering challenges and continuing to develop as an engineer capable of contributing to the design and development of high-performance mechanical systems.
